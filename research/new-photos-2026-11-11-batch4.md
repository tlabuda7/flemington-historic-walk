## Findings that touch built or planned things

1. **`flemington_-_womens_rest_room_-_1925.jpg` = the Wurts Law Office (59 Main) in 1925**, as
   the "Women's Rest Room". It shows a **different state from the "Dr. Tompkins" card**:
   a pale **solid panelled door**, a pale board over the door, one shutter visible (outer right),
   and a **rough fieldstone wall with a jagged coping along the sidewalk** with a concrete walk
   through a gap, instead of the wire fence. The Tompkins card is undated (est. 1910-25), and on
   2026-11-11 the model was changed to match it (half-glazed door, "DR. TOMPKINS" board).
   **Open question: which state is right for 1920-21?** Needs dates: when Dr. Tompkins used the
   office and when the Women's Rest Room opened and the wall was built.
2. **`flmnhtnwvfishergrocerydelivwagon10.jpg`** (1910): a real Flemington delivery wagon:
   closed-top grocery wagon with open sides, light carriage wheels, lettered "W. V. FISHER /
   GROCER / We Give S&H Green Stamps / Flemington, N.J.". The wagon in the scene is a plain
   open box (typical, unlettered). This is photo evidence for a different type.
3. **`flemington_-_court_street_-_cars_in_a_parade_-_c_1910.jpg`**: Court Street itself: heavy
   street trees both sides, earth road, a curbed walk and iron fence on one side, a white fence
   on the other, a pale building beyond. First real view for the Court St backdrop.
4. **`flemington_-_main_street_-_vosseller_building_-_1907.jpg`**: Main St with the "Vosseller
   Building" (three storeys, two-tier porch) at the right, a wide grass strip and concrete walk
   at the left. Location to be placed on the Sanborn sheets.
5. **`flmngtnyetanotherparade.jpg`**: marchers on Main St past shop awnings and a porch with
   cresting (Union Hotel?). **`flmngtnparadefloatbldg.jpg`**: a float in front of the stepped
   concrete-block garage (G1), a close view of its wall and stepped parapet.
6. **`flmngtnunionhtllindberghview.jpg`** (1935) and HABS **`union_hotel_-_76_main_street…`**
   (1960s-70s): later Union Hotel views, useful for porch detail only.
7. **`flemington_-_main_street_north_from_bloomfield_avenue_-_flemington_garage_-_c_1910.jpg`**:
   a second scan of the Rea Building / "FLEMINGTON GARAGE CO." card.
