## Findings that touch built or planned things

1. **`flemington_-_womens_rest_room_-_1925.jpg` = the Wurts Law Office (59 Main) in 1925**, as
   the "Women's Rest Room". It shows a **different state from the "Dr. Tompkins" card**:
   a pale **solid panelled door**, a pale board over the door, one shutter visible (outer right),
   and a **rough fieldstone wall with a jagged coping along the sidewalk** with a concrete walk
   through a gap, instead of the wire fence. The Tompkins card is undated (est. 1910-25), and on
   2026-11-11 the model was changed to match it (half-glazed door, "DR. TOMPKINS" board).
   **Open question: which state is right for 1920-21?** Needs dates: when Dr. Tompkins used the
   office and when the Women's Rest Room opened and the wall was built.
2. **`flmnhtnwvfishergrocerydelivwagon10.jpg`** (1910): a real Flemington delivery wagon:
   closed-top grocery wagon with open sides, light carriage wheels, lettered "W. V. FISHER /
   GROCER / We Give S&H Green Stamps / Flemington, N.J.". The wagon in the scene is a plain
   open box (typical, unlettered). This is photo evidence for a different type.
3. **`flemington_-_court_street_-_cars_in_a_parade_-_c_1910.jpg`**: Court Street itself: heavy
   street trees both sides, earth road, a curbed walk and iron fence on one side, a white fence
   on the other, a pale building beyond. First real view for the Court St backdrop.
4. **`flemington_-_main_street_-_vosseller_building_-_1907.jpg`**: Main St with the "Vosseller
   Building" (three storeys, two-tier porch) at the right, a wide grass strip and concrete walk
   at the left. Location to be placed on the Sanborn sheets.
5. **`flmngtnyetanotherparade.jpg`**: marchers on Main St past shop awnings and a porch with
   cresting (Union Hotel?). **`flmngtnparadefloatbldg.jpg`**: a float in front of the stepped
   concrete-block garage (G1), a close view of its wall and stepped parapet.
6. **`flmngtnunionhtllindberghview.jpg`** (1935) and HABS **`union_hotel_-_76_main_street…`**
   (1960s-70s): later Union Hotel views, useful for porch detail only.
7. **`flemington_-_main_street_north_from_bloomfield_avenue_-_flemington_garage_-_c_1910.jpg`**:
   a second scan of the Rea Building / "FLEMINGTON GARAGE CO." card.

## Planner pass (2026-11-11)

All named photos viewed at full size (crops for the Wurts door/wall, wagon lettering, Court St sides, Vosseller
building, parade porch, float sign); 1910 sheets 4–5 and 1921 sheet 3 checked with crops; LoC HABS search via the
pictures JSON API.

1. **Wurts Law Office (59 Main): keep the Tompkins state.** Rest room opened **1923** (Woman's Club history page;
   Vosseller, Hunterdon Democrat, 26 July 1923, via Goodspeed Histories). Dr. Tompkins: no source found (4 searches).
   The 1925 card's pale panelled door matches the HABS (1960s) white door, so the dark half-glazed Tompkins door came
   first; the fieldstone wall most likely came with the 1923 conversion (guess). ≈ 80 % the Tompkins card predates
   1923; the "DR. TOMPKINS" board itself is the only uncertain item (his years unknown). Wurts addendum (batch 4) lists
   the exact changes if the owner prefers the 1925 state; a mix is not recommended. Pass-2 fence row kept (note added).
2. **W. V. Fisher wagon (1910)**: canopy-top grocer's delivery wagon (closed rear box body, open-sided front under a
   bowed roof extending over the seat, elliptic springs, tall thin-spoked pale wheels, dark body). Lettering on the rear
   side panel: "W. V. FISHER" (arched) / "GROCER" / "WE GIVE "S&H" GREEN STAMPS" / "[FLE]MINGTON, N.J.". Shop location not
   found (no Sanborn proprietor names; web nothing). Keep the open wagon at F14; optional second wagon of this type,
   unlettered, at 35–37 Main. PLAN pass 2, 8a.
3. **Court St card (c. 1910)**: looks **east** from near Park Ave (≈ 60 %): park lawn with a lamp pole and a white
   X-braced rail on the north (left), fenced house fronts with a stone-curbed walk on the south (right), earth road, big
   maples meeting overhead; the pale gabled house is beyond the park (not identified). PLAN 7.8 and row K6–K10.
4. **"Vosseller Building" = 108–112 Main (E5)**, the 1921 "Stationery" block (E. Vosseller, Stationer, published cards);
   1907 card looks north from south of Higgins Ave; its 2nd-storey porch is on the 1921 map ("PORCH 2D"). Street end,
   not the walkable stretch; 106 Main too small in the card to compare. Row E5 now PHOTO-BASED.
5. **`flmngtnyetanotherparade.jpg`** (undated, c. 1905–15 by dress): Main St at the **Union Hotel**, camera on the road
   near Court St looking **north**: the hotel's 1-storey porch with its lattice roof railing and the arched centre on the

   the west curb. Agrees with the Union Hotel model; no change. **`flmngtnparadefloatbldg.jpg`** (c. 1910): close view of
   G1's Spring St front: rock-faced block, pale sills and lintels, 3-step parapet each side, one bordered cast sign panel
