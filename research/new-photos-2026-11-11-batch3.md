## Findings that touch built or planned things

1. **`flmngtnmainstdrtompkinsoffice.jpg` = the Wurts Law Office (59 Main)** in period use
   as "DR. TOMPKINS" office. PLAN said no West Jersey photo shows it; this one does, close up.
   Shows: square pillars, wreath frieze, flush-board front, **open louvred shutters on both
   front windows**, 6-over-6 sash, half-glazed door with a small name board over it, a
   concrete walk to the step, and a **low wire hoop-top fence with square white posts and an
   urn finial at the gate** (not a picket fence, as pass 2 currently guesses for 59).
   To check against the model: shutters, door, fence type.
2. **`flmngtnbizblocks12.jpg`** ("Some Business Blocks", 1912): east side looking south-east:
   Nevius store (78), Odd Fellows Hall (82-84), then the **Masonic Hall before its 1921 stone
   front** (brick, gabled, arched windows, awnings). Odd Fellows matches the model (7 windows
   per floor, curved roof arch, clapboard north side with one window per floor, two awnings).
   Sharper than the 1907 card for the Nevius shopfront (scalloped awning valance, bracketed
   shop cornice) and the hitching/mounting stones at the curb.
3. **`flmngtnfirehs28.jpg`** (1928 "Fire House" card, same view as the Goodspeed one): stucco,
   two storeys, recessed upper porch behind square piers, pedimented gable with a round date
   plaque, "FLEMINGTON FIRE DEPT" over two bays with glazed folding doors. PLAN 7.1 says this
   is the former Lyceum after the borough bought it: planner to settle whether that was
   already so in 1920-21.
4. **`flmngtndeatsbldg08.jpg`** ("Free Library - Deats Building", 1908): three-storey brick
   block with a pedimented centre, stone window heads, four chimneys and a shop front,
   **directly beside the Methodist church**. Likely the E11 block of the street-ends plan.
5. **`flmngtncountyhtl06.jpg`** (colour, 1906): County Hotel painted tan/buff with green
   shutters, plain two-tier porch, lamp post at the corner. Colour check for the model.
6. **`flmngtncrthslindbrgtrl.jpg`** (1935): court house north flank, chimneys and side
   windows. Later than 1920, but good for the side wall.
7. **Two hotels not in the plans**: `flmngtnmainstrtvuwthhotel08.jpg` /
   `flmngtnhotelhntrdon00s10s.jpg` ("Main Street looking south from Hotel Hunterdon": brick,
   three-tier gingerbread porch) and `flmngtnhtlflemingtonvu08.jpg` ("Hotel Flemington":
   frame, long two-tier porch with gabled entrance hoods). **Not identified yet**: planner to
   place them on the Sanborn sheets (could be other names for known buildings).
