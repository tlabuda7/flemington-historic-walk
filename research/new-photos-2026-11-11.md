# New owner photos, 2026-11-11 (second batch)

Planner, 2026-11-11. 26 West Jersey History postcards saved by hand by the owner into
`assets/photos/reference/` (**local only, gitignored: never commit or upload them**; referenced
here by file name only). Each was viewed full size, with enlarged / contrast-stretched crops in
the session scratchpad (not kept). Positions use the Sept 1921 Sanborn sheets 2 and 3 and the
Nov 1910 sheet 4 in `assets/maps/` (registrations as in `research/sanborn-and-today-findings.md`
and PLAN.md pass 2 section 9.1). No web research was done for this note.

Short verdict: **the new cards matter for the side-streets / street-ends backdrop** (the
Flemington Garage, the park band stand, both churches, the monument, the Doric House, the public
library); they show **no real contradiction with any built Main St building**. Only the 1909
Bloomfield Ave card adds Main St detail (upper-window awnings and the "FLEMINGTON GARAGE CO." sign
on the Rea Building, both 1909, and a barber-pole ball).

## Side streets and street ends

### flmingtngarage09.jpg — "Flemington Garage, Flemington, N. J." (1909)
- **Where**: the **cement-block garage on the SE corner of Bloomfield Ave and Spring St**, Nov 1910
  sheet 4 nos. 317–318: blue (stone/concrete) box lettered "GARAGE. CEMENT FL. IR. RF.", "CEMENT
  BLOCK CONSTR'N", "1", "14'", front rooms "Office" (317) and "Repair Shop" (318) along Bloomfield
  Ave, "Gasol. Eng.". **Not** the 2-storey brick garage at 21 Bloomfield (PLAN B6), which is new
  between 1910 and 1921 and stands west of Spring St. Registered to scene coordinates (sheet 4 →
  sheet 2: x + 3392, y + 780; west wall snapped to the sheet-2 Spring St east line, x 6180):
  **x 124.2 … 145.8, z −77.6 … −55.7** (±1.5 m), about 21.6 × 21.9 m. The east part of the
  block is drawn only on sheet 4, so sheet 2 shows the corner empty ("4" in the block).
- **Viewpoint**: from the Bloomfield / Spring crossing, north-west of the building, looking
  south-east. Left (receding): the **north side on Bloomfield Ave**; right (nearly face-on): the
  **west side on Spring St**. Far left, 3 m east of it: the 2-storey gable-fronted frame building
  (1910 nos. 319–320 "Storage, Painting, Wheelwright, Bl. Sm.") with its own sign board.
- **Shows**: one storey (about 4.3 m, map 14'), **rock-faced concrete block** in even courses,
  light buff-grey; smooth pale concrete sills and lintels. Flat-topped walls with a plain
  parapet on the north part; over the **south part of the Spring St side a stepped gable
  parapet** (3 steps each side and a flat top, peak about 6 m), with a **pale sign panel** in it
  (two lines of lettering, **not legible** at this resolution) and a grey pitched iron roof
  showing behind it. Spring St side, left to right: 2 windows and a plain door (notices on it),
  a smaller window, a large multi-pane window, an **open garage door (≈ 3 m wide) with a car
  inside**, one more window. Bloomfield side: about 6 evenly spaced windows, a **long band of
  lettering along the parapet** (not legible), a round sign. Two chimneys (one tall, mid-roof).
  Earth road, a wooden pole at the corner, a granolithic walk along Spring St. Men in the doorway.
- **Changes**: new backdrop item **G1** (PLAN side streets, section 7). It shows only as a
  sliver from Main St (its west face is hidden behind no. 23 / B7 except near the corner), so
  tier 2. **Whether it was still a garage in 1921 is not known locally** (check the Sept 1921
  sheet 4 at LoC if wanted).

### flempreschch23.jpg — "Presbyterian Church, Flemington, N. J." (1923)
- **Where / viewpoint**: from the south, across a landscaped green, looking north at the
  church's south front. Closest to 1920 of all church views.
- **Shows**: grey stone Gothic church; **tall slender spire with small gabled lucarnes and a
  weathervane at the left (south-west) end**, its square tower hidden by trees; on the front a
  large **pointed-arch window**, to its right a **row of five small round-headed windows** in a
  lower range, a **round window high in the main gable**, small roof gables, dark slate roofs.
  In front: lawn with a **curving gravel path, flower beds and young conifers**, tall bare trees
  that hide most of the church. At the left edge a **raised porch with a heavy square newel, a
  balustrade and a lattice skirt** — it looks like the corner of a house porch rather than an
  octagonal band stand; it is **not** the Court St park band stand (that one stands about 350 m
  away, behind the courthouse).
- **Changes**: E8 form confirmed for c. 1920; spire at the west end confirmed. Add the
  landscaped lawn and trees in front (tier 2: a few instanced trees).

### flemmethchch09.jpg (colour, 1909) and flemngtnmethchch06.jpg (1906) — Methodist Church
- **Where / viewpoints**: 116 Main, SE corner of Main St and Maple Ave (E7). 1906: from Main St,
  west of the church, looking east; the street on the right is Maple Ave. 1909: from the SW
  (Main / Maple corner), looking NE, across the grass plot at the corner.
- **Shows**: rock-faced stone in regular courses, **warm buff-grey** (1909 colour card) with
  paler dressed bands; **two steep gables** (west to Main St, south to Maple Ave), each with a
  **large pointed-arch window with a rose (plate tracery) in its head**; **square tower in the
  angle between them** with a pointed-arch door at the foot up a few steps, a small pointed window,
  a corbel band, a belfry with **two tall louvred lancets per face**, **four corner pinnacles**
  and a steep pyramidal spire with a cross; low east wing with a hipped / pyramidal roof and a
  small finial; crosses on the gables. 1909: **thick ivy over the whole west gable**, roofs
  coloured green by the card colourist (real slate colour not proven), young trees along the
  walk, a grass plot with a curved curb in front. 1906: bare trees, a thin lamp post at the walk
  in front of the tower, poles along Maple Ave, Queen Anne houses beyond.
- **Changes**: E7 confirmed and given colour, ivy, door steps and the lamp post. The 1906 card
  also shows the Doric House (below).

### flemington_-_library_building.jpg — "Flemington Public Library" (undated, c. 1910s)
- **Where**: 118 Main, **SE corner of Main St and Maple Ave**. Sept 1921 sheet 3: brick, "2",
  "28'", "PUBLIC LIBRARY", box x 4150–4462, y 3893–4103 → scene **x 16.8 … 33.1, z 136.3 …
  147.3** (10.9 m on Main St, 16.3 m on Maple Ave, front 6.5 m behind the Main St line).
- **Viewpoint**: most likely from Maple Ave, NE of the building, looking SW (the lettered 4-bay
  face is the long side, so it is the Maple Ave side; the 3-bay face is then the east end).
  Orientation is a guess.
- **Shows**: 2 storeys, dark red brick with **pale stone pilasters** at the corners and between
  bays, stone base and band courses, **frieze lettered "FLEMINGTON PUBLIC LIBRARY"** on the
  4-bay face, a projecting cornice and a low hipped roof; ground-floor windows paired 1/1 with
  stone heads, **brick relieving arches** over them; upper windows 6/6 with stone sills; a door
  with a transom and steps on each face. Walk with a stone curb, earth road, a young tree.
- **Note**: the 3-storey "Club Room / Post Office" block that the 1921 map draws 4 m south of the
  library does not show above its roof, so the card probably predates that block.
- **Changes**: new street-end item **E10** (seen past the Methodist church's west front from the
  west sidewalk at the south walk limit). Also from sheet 3: new item **E11**, the Club Room /
  **Post Office** block (120–124 Main), 3 storeys brick, "40'", meeting halls on the 3rd floor,
  x 10.8 … 37.4, z 151.2 … 160.2, on the street line. **This is where the Post Office had moved
  by Sept 1921** (the Rea Building spec says "where it went is unknown").

## Built buildings: contradictions and photo-backed additions

**No real contradiction was found.** Photo-backed points, for the owner to choose:

1. **Rea Building, upper-window awnings** (`flmngntnmainstandbloomfieldaveview09.jpg`, 1909):
   canvas awnings on several 2nd-floor windows (Main front near the corner, Bloomfield side). The
   spec left the "1906–07 upper-window awnings" out; this card shows they were still used in
   1909, but there is still no 1920 evidence. Suggest: **leave out** (or, optional: 3 plain
   cream awnings on the 2nd-floor windows nearest the corner, ~+150 tris).
2. **Rea Building, "FLEMINGTON / GARAGE CO." arrow sign** (same card, 1909): points to the
   cement-block garage on Bloomfield / Spring, which the 1910 map shows. Whether the company and
   the sign still existed in 1920 is unknown (the 1921 sheet 2 does not show that corner; the
   new 21 Bloomfield garage is a different building). Suggest: **leave out** (no 1920 evidence).
3. **Rea Building, barber-pole ball** (same card): a white ball on a post at the middle of the
   front = the barber pole with a ball top of the Stryker plate and the 1910 card. Already
   covered by pass-2 decision 7. No fix.
4. **Rea Building, Post Office** (map, not photo): the Sept 1921 sheet 3 shows the **Post Office
   at 124 Main** (with a Club Room at 120–122 and meeting halls upstairs). The spec's "where it
   went is unknown" can be answered. **Text-only fix in PLAN.md; nothing to change in the model**
   (it already shows no P.O.).
5. **Block 40–46 and pass-2 F5**: the 1909 card shows one big full-crowned tree at the curb in
   front of 44–46, as F5 proposes. Confirms F5; no fix to the building.
6. **County Hotel** (`flmngntnmainstcountyhtl08.jpg`, 1908): full veranda, bracketed cornice,
   flat top, 35–37's two-tier porch beyond — all as built. No fix.
7. **57 Main**: none of the new cards shows the 1921 shop fronts (the 1908 card's far-left wall
   is unreadable; the 1909 view looks away from it). The built Sept 1921 state stands. No fix.
8. **Union Hotel, 24–30, 32–38**: not shown in any new card (the 1910 float card's veranda is
   not identifiable). No fix.
9. **Street details (pass 2)**: every new street view (1908, 1909, 1910, 1913, 1914, c. 1912)
   shows a **bare earth road**, wooden poles with crossarms, street trees in plain pits and,
   on residential streets, white picket fences — all consistent with pass 2 (earth road, F5,
   F8, picket fence for 59 / 65 Main). No fix.
